What the report concerns

Niezalezna.pl attributed a claim about foreigners’ involvement in the shooting to Sakiewicz. TV Republika described the information as unofficial. In Onet’s later live coverage, police said the person detained was a Polish citizen and the only known perpetrator.

The author asked prosecutors to preserve the full broadcast segment and examine whether the statement could constitute an offence under Article 256 § 1 of the Polish Criminal Code. The assessment rests with the competent authorities.
